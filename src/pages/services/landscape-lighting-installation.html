Before installation

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Do you install landscape lighting designed by someone else?

Yes. We install our own designs and designs prepared by others. Share the plan during your consultation so we can review it alongside your property and agree the installation scope.

Can you install lighting equipment I already own?

Yes. Customer-supplied equipment is part of our installation service. Tell us what you have so we can review compatibility and condition, discuss any limitations, and agree what will be installed before work begins.

How do you install lighting around existing planting and hardscape?

We plan wiring routes and fixture placement around your planting, lawn and hardscape. The methods depend on the property and the agreed system. We discuss access and any areas needing particular care during the consultation.

What happens during the evening lighting demo?

We set up a temporary demonstration at dusk so you can experience the proposed lighting on your property. It helps us discuss brightness, focal points and the overall effect before installation.

How much does landscape lighting installation cost?

Focused front-façade installation packages start at $5,000. Comprehensive estate installations typically range from $15,000 to $50,000, depending on acreage and architectural complexity. Your proposal sets the scope and pricing; projects using outside designs or customer-supplied equipment are priced individually.

Do you install landscape lighting in McLean, Great Falls and nearby areas?

Yes. We serve McLean, Great Falls, Vienna and Reston in Virginia, plus Potomac, Cabin John and Bethesda in Maryland. We welcome other DMV inquiries; share your address so we can confirm availability.

What happens after the lights are installed?

We fine-tune the aiming at dusk and walk you through the lighting and controls. This final visit lets us review the finished effect with you and bring the individual elements together.

How long will my landscape lighting installation take?

Timing depends on the property, access and agreed scope. We discuss the schedule for your project rather than promising a fixed installation time before reviewing the site.
