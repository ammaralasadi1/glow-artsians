Where do you provide holiday lighting services?

Our focus areas are McLean, Great Falls, Vienna, Oakton, and Reston in Virginia, plus Potomac, Cabin John, and Bethesda in Maryland. We also welcome inquiries from other Northern Virginia homeowners. Contact us to confirm availability for your address.

What does the all-inclusive service cover?

Design, installation, seasonal maintenance, post-holiday takedown, and storage for your agreed exterior display. The lighting, decorations, and areas included in your project are confirmed during the consultation.

Do you offer indoor Christmas decorating?

No. This service focuses on exterior Christmas lighting and outdoor holiday decorations, including rooflines, trees, pathways, and entrance décor.

When should I plan my holiday display?

Contact us when you’re ready to start planning, especially if you have a preferred installation date. We’ll discuss current availability during your consultation; a specific date is not guaranteed until confirmed.

What happens if part of the display stops working?

Seasonal maintenance is included for the display we install. Contact Glow Artisans so our team can review the issue and arrange the next steps.

Can I inquire if I live elsewhere in Northern Virginia?

Absolutely. Share your property address and the exterior areas you’d like decorated so we can confirm service availability and discuss your ideas.
