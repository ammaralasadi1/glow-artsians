Do you install Christmas lights in Great Falls, VA?

Yes. Glow Artisans offers residential exterior Christmas lighting and holiday decorating in Great Falls, Virginia. Start with a design consultation so we can review your address, display ideas, and installation timing.

What does the all-inclusive service cover?

Design, installation, seasonal maintenance, post-holiday takedown, and storage for your agreed exterior display. The lighting, decorations, and areas included in your project are confirmed during the consultation.

Do you offer indoor Christmas decorating?

No. This service focuses on exterior Christmas lighting and outdoor holiday decorations, including rooflines, trees, pathways, and entrance décor.

Can the driveway and house be part of one display?

Yes. We can discuss the driveway approach, selected trees, roofline, and front entrance together. The areas included depend on your design, available power, and installation access; we confirm the scope during your Great Falls consultation.

What happens if part of the display stops working?

Seasonal maintenance is included for the display we install. Contact Glow Artisans so our team can review the issue and arrange the next steps.

Can I inquire if I live elsewhere in Northern Virginia?

Absolutely. Great Falls is the focus of this page, but we welcome inquiries from homeowners elsewhere in Northern Virginia. Share your property address so we can confirm whether we can serve your home.
